The objective of the project is to analyze the demographic situation across Ukrainian regions and identify a potential relationship between income levels and demographic indicators, particularly birth rates, mortality rates, and natural population growth.

## Object of Research
Demographic processes across Ukrainian regions in 2020–2021.

## Subject of Research
Indicators: birth rate, mortality rate, natural population growth, average salary, and the gender structure of births and deaths.

## Key Project Tasks
Collect and prepare statistical data; analyze birth and mortality rates across regions; calculate natural population growth rates; identify regions with the best and worst demographic situations; examine the relationship between income levels and demographic indicators; and build visualizations in Tableau Public.

## Data Used
The project uses official statistical data for 2020 and 2021 on population, birth rates, mortality rates, and average salaries across Ukrainian regions.

Section: Analysis of Demographic Rates and Regional Differences

A comparative analysis of demographic indicators for 2020 and 2021 shows a significant deterioration in Ukraine’s natural population growth. Two interconnected trends can be observed across all regions of the country:

an increase in the Death Rate;
a decrease in the Birth Rate.

The combination of these factors indicates an acceleration of population decline.

## Conclusion:

The analysis shows that the demographic situation in Ukraine deteriorated significantly in 2021 compared with 2020. Across all regions, birth rates declined, mortality rates increased, and the population decreased, indicating an acceleration of the depopulation process.

The additional correlation analysis did not reveal a strong linear relationship between average salary levels and demographic indicators. This suggests that income levels alone do not explain regional differences in birth and mortality rates.

The results highlight the need for a comprehensive approach to demographic analysis, taking into account economic, social, healthcare, and migration factors. Such an approach can provide a deeper understanding of the drivers behind demographic changes and serve as a basis for further research and data-driven decision-making.

## Recommendations for Further Analysis

Investigate the impact of migration on population changes; assess the relationship between the age structure of the population and mortality rates; analyze the relationship between demographic indicators, employment levels, and regional GRP; and develop predictive models to forecast demographic changes in the coming years.

All visualizations and analysis results are available in Tableau Public and can be used for further research on the demographic situation in Ukraine.
